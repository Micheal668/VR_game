The project removes the printable stand, scales and simplifies the mesh for VR,
assigns materials, groups the visible stages, and opens an access hatch and
window. The interior, crew seating and cinematic staging are project artwork.
The color and shape references include the NASA Space Launch System Program
Office display model: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/space-launch-system-block-1/

https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
